# LINAD — учебная программа German и English

План ≠ опубликованный урок. Цели (Outcome) и фокусы — редакционные метаданные на английском; учебный материал переводится на язык поддержки при публикации.

## DE A1 · 1. Знакомство и глагол sein

1. **Hallo, ich bin …** (first-introductions) — опубликован.
   Focus: sein; personal pronouns; greetings.
   Outcome: Introduce yourself and recognise who is new in a class.

2. **Wie heisst du?** (introduction-questions) — опубликован.
   Focus: W-questions; yes/no questions; du/Sie.
   Outcome: Ask and answer simple identity questions politely.

3. **Woher kommst du?** (a1-introductions-3) — опубликован.
   Focus: kommen/wohnen in the singular; aus/in with cities and countries.
   Outcome: Distinguish origin from current residence in a short exchange.

4. **Meine Kontaktdaten** (a1-introductions-4) — опубликован.
   Focus: numbers 0–20; spelling; heissen; formal questions.
   Outcome: Read and complete a basic name and contact form.

5. **Ein kurzes Profil** (a1-introductions-5) — опубликован.
   Focus: simple clauses with und; pronoun reference; introductory message.
   Outcome: Read a class profile and assemble a short coherent introduction.
